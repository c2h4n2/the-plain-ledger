About The Plain Ledger

Most personal finance sites are trying to sell you something — a course, an app subscription, a credit card with an affiliate link attached. The Plain Ledger doesn't have a product. It's a small, independent site with one job: lay out the numbers clearly enough that you can make your own decision.

What you'll find here

Plain-English guides on budgeting, saving, and debt payoff, written the way you'd explain it to a friend rather than the way a textbook would. Alongside the guides, a handful of free calculators — no account required, nothing saved, nothing sold.

What we're not

We're not licensed financial advisors, and nothing on this site should be taken as personalized financial, legal, or tax advice. We're a resource for general education and simple math — for decisions specific to your situation, a qualified professional (a fee-only financial planner, a CPA, a credit counselor) is worth the conversation.

How the site is funded

The Plain Ledger is supported by advertising, which helps keep every guide and calculator free and open to anyone. Ads are served through Google AdSense and are clearly separated from editorial content.

Questions or corrections

If something's unclear, out of date, or just wrong, we'd genuinely like to know. Reach out via the contact page.
